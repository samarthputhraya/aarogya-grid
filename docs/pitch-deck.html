Aarogya Grid

The medicine is already there.
Nobody can see it.

A federated early-warning grid for India's primary health network. Demand is forecast at every facility–drug position — on Google's TimesFM wherever a held-out backtest says it wins — the detector spots an outbreak surge days before a shelf empties, and the planner moves medicine across district lines before it does, without creating a stock-out anywhere else.

Problem
The problem

A sub-centre runs out of anti-snake venom in monsoon. The vials exist.

They are ninety minutes away, in a facility that will write them off unused in six weeks. Nobody knows either fact.

Both facilities report into a paper register that reaches the district office weeks later, if at all. India's public health supply chain does not primarily fail on procurement volume — it fails on visibility, and on lateral movement: the ability to see a shortage forming and move stock sideways before it becomes a stock-out.

The loop
Who it serves, and what closes

Four users, one dataset — and a loop that comes back.

ANMSub-centre
Speaks one sentence into a phone instead of filling a register. Told at once if what she reported looks wrong.
StorekeeperPHC / CHC
Gets an order naming a batch, a quantity, an expiry and a distance. Nothing to interpret.
District Health OfficerDistrict
Asks in their own language, gets a grounded answer with its working shown, and signs what can move.
State & ministryNational
Sees which districts fail structurally rather than unluckily — and gets a national demand model without any state handing over its facility rows.
OBSERVE
Gemini transcribes spoken Hindi or reads a photographed register. A human confirms before anything is committed.
PREDICT
TimesFM forecasts 21 days ahead; a Monte Carlo over the lead time turns that into a stock-out probability and an expected shortfall.
RECOMMEND
A dispatch order: named batch, expiry date, distance, price — bounded by what the batch physically holds.
EXECUTE
approve → dispatch → receive, on an append-only audit trail. A short receipt is a variance, not a rounding.
MONITOR
Re-scored server-side in 14 ms and pushed over SSE. Survives a reload and a killed container.
Most systems of this shape stop at Recommend. Nothing afterwards comes back, so nobody learns that 14 vials were sent and 11 arrived. Here the receiver's risk recovers by what turned up.

Solution
The output

Not a dashboard alert. A dispatch order a storekeeper can act on.

Generated dispatch order · South West Khasi Hills → West Khasi Hills, Meghalaya
SC West Khasi Hills-02 holds 1 sachet of oral rehydration salts — a Vital drug — against a 100% chance of running short inside its 15-day resupply window. CHC South West Khasi Hills-01, in the next district, can spare 107. Moving them 40.2 km costs ₹1,174 and averts an expected 86.0 sachets of unmet demand, cutting stock-out risk from 100% to 4%.
pick list: B012-HC001 × 23 (exp 2028-11-07)  ·  B013-HC001 × 84 (exp 2029-04-29)  ·  risk reduction 96 pp  ·  donor left at 0% stock-out risk

Every recommendation names the specific batches and their expiry dates, oldest first, and the quantity is bounded by what those batches physically hold. A recommendation a storekeeper cannot fill is not a recommendation. This one crosses a district boundary — the clause the brief adds this edition — so it also carries who is allowed to issue it: West Khasi Hills cannot approve it alone, and the console disables Approve until South West Khasi Hills countersigns. This order pays for its own vehicle — ₹1,174 planned, ₹1,174 alone — and that vehicle then carries 3 other orders that ride along for the price of handling.

06
AI approach
How Google AI is used, and how it is bounded

TimesFM forecasts. Gemini reads and retrieves. Neither invents a number.

TIMESFM 2.0
All 36,143 district × drug series, 21 days ahead from a 90-day context, through BigQuery AI.FORECAST — 13 concurrent statements, 0 bytes billed, none declined.
GEMINI
Spoken Hindi and photographed registers into structured entries; and a function-calling surface of 12 tools over the computed state, with the tool trace on screen.
DETERMINISTIC
Censored Croston-SBA, Monte Carlo risk over each facility's own 7–18 day resupply window, and the redistribution optimiser. Reproducible from a seed.
NEVER THE MODEL
No arithmetic, no drug code, no facility id, no status. The drug is resolved server-side from its name, never from a client-supplied id.
CENSORING
A ledger records what was dispensed, not what was needed — a stock-out looks exactly like a quiet week. Fitting naively under-orders for the facilities already failing, so the fit excludes stocked-out periods.

07
Emergencies
Health emergencies

A stock-out is a lagging indicator. We publish how often the warning is wrong.

By the time a block's anti-malarials run short, the malaria has been there a fortnight. AI.DETECT_ANOMALIES runs over district consumption and OPD attendance — 36,912 series, 32 statements, 0 bytes billed — and a warning is a rule over points, tuned against 126 injected surges and scored on 80 candidate rules.

21% precision is not a good number and it is on this slide anyway, next to the 78 rules that failed. Two tighter rules reach 53% and 42% and miss only the four-day lead. Moving the gate after seeing the table would turn every number here into an argument.

The measurement inverted the obvious expectation. Footfall is upstream and nearly blind at district scale: an outbreak doubles one disease, and vector-borne illness is ~9% of a district's OPD, so a 2× surge arrives in total attendance as ×1.09 while the drugs that treat it double. The tuned rule runs on consumption.

Each state fits its own model and publishes a monthly demand multiplier per catalogue item, its standard error, and one vacancy rate per cadre. Pooled by random effects with the between-state variance estimated from the nodes, so a state keeps its own estimate exactly to the extent its data earns it.

Checkable, not assertable. GET /api/federated/10 returns Bihar's node byte for byte; the index publishes the SHA-256 of all thirty-six. npm test sweeps every file for facility ids, district codes and batch numbers — after first proving on five poisoned copies that the sweep can still see one.

Finding
The honest economics

It does not pay for itself in cash. It pays in shortfall, and we lead with the sign.

National planValue
Waste averted+ ₹18.9 L

It breaks even at ₹3.11 per averted unit of unmet demand. Whether a dose of a Vital medicine reaching a patient is worth that is a policy judgement, not an engineering one — so the shortage penalty is an explicit parameter, not something folded into a headline.

Visibility was not the binding constraint. A truck per tablet was. Of 34,576 needs the planner declined, 97.9% failed the benefit/cost gate — the medicine existed, a median 19.9 km away, and the trip cost more than the medicine. Only 8 failed for want of stock anywhere.

Provenance
Real vs simulated

Stated plainly, because the honest answer is the stronger one.

Real  769 districts at their headquarters towns, with LGD codes · Census 2011 district populations, reconciled to state totals · the NLEM drug catalogue, forms, strengths and VED classes · IPHS tier, bed and staffing norms · an NFHS-5 indicator for every state · the national boundary from DataMeet's composite file.

Simulated  Facility-level stock, consumption, batches and expiries · bed occupancy · staff attendance · OPD footfall. Labelled SIMULATED FACILITY DATA in the console header and on the landing page, and itemised in NOTICE.

Why simulated? Because the absence of this data is the problem. If per-facility stock across India's PHC network were available as an extract, the shortage would already be visible. Three adapter functions stand between this and a DVDMS or e-Aushadhi extract; nothing downstream changes.
OWN IT
No authentication. Writes are server-side only; the actor is recorded as actor_claimed, which is what it is.
OWN IT
--max-instances=1 during judging: the live overlay is in-process. The scale-out step is a subscriber on a topic we already publish to, deliberately not built.
OWN IT
The federated τ² is synthetic — one simulator behind all thirty-six.
OWN IT
The batch is a script, not a scheduled job. One machine, run by hand.
Nothing on any surface is typed by a human. A drift guard re-derives every figure in this deck, the README and the defence pack from the shipped payloads on every npm test. It has caught a slide quoting a dispatch order that no longer existed.

Next
Pilot, scale, and portability

One state, eight weeks, two metrics — then the same code, more rows.

WEEK 1–2
Point the three adapters at one state's DVDMS/e-Aushadhi extract. Positioned as a decision layer over DVDMS, e-Aushadhi and IHIP — not a replacement.
WEEK 3–6
Two districts live: the ANM capture path, the DHO console, dispatch tickets in the district office.
WEEK 7–8
Measure two things and nothing else: stock-out days prevented, and time from alert to action.
REACH
2,348 district-to-district corridors carry medicine in the shipped plan, 284 cross-state corridors among them — each one classified, and each one refusing to be approved by a district officer alone.
SCALE
All 769 districts in 900–1,300 s on one laptop, measured rather than extrapolated. District-parallel with no shared state; planning, which shares stock, colours into 10 rounds of disjoint clusters, not 769 tasks.
BRICS portability. The core is country-agnostic: forecasting, censored fitting, risk, and a distance-and-price optimiser. What is country-specific is a medicines list, facility norms, a currency and a language pack — four adapters, not a rewrite.

The early-warning feed already leaves in a schema nobody has to be Indian to read: an area has a code, a named code system, a name and a population; a signal has a hazard class from a fixed list, an observed value, an expected range and a confidence. Every Indian identifier travels in an optional block a consumer can drop, and npm test strips that block and re-validates.

What we would build next, in order
A Pub/Sub subscriber so the live overlay survives more than one instance · a real IDSP/IHIP bulletin ingest · per-state backtests once two states have real history · and the thing a ministry will ask for first: procurement forecasting, which is the same demand model pointed a quarter further out.
